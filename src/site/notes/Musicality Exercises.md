# Tempow

- Play different kinds of mainstream music and try to find the tempo by clapping, counting, or tapping your foot.
- Try to find the tempo even when there is no clear beat. Keep counting to check if your tempo is correct; if it is, your count should match the beat once it starts.

# Diagram

The step diagram consists of numbers that indicate how long you should wait before taking the next step, based on the tempo.

- 0 → Pause
- 1 → Step on every beat
- 2 → Step every 2 beats
- 4 → Step every 4 beats

**Note:** 1 + 0 is equivalent to 2

*Examples:*
- 1 1 1 1 → step . step . step . step
- 1 1 2 → step . step . step . pause
- 2 2 → step . pause . step . pause
- 4 → step . pause . pause . pause
- 0 0 2 → pause . pause . step . pause

# Pause

Start by using a metronome at 60 BPM, then increase to 90 BPM, and finally 130 BPM.

- Start walking.
- Pick a number between 1 and 32.
- When you’re ready, stop — that moment will be your “1.” Then count up to your chosen number, following the tempo.
- Resume walking.

*Notes:*

- The goal is to get used to waiting and to develop patience.
- Make sure your step lands on count “1.”
- Counting out loud is easier at first; once you’re comfortable, switch to counting mentally.
- Once you’re comfortable with the metronome, try using songs from any genre and different speeds.
