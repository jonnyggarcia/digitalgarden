# Flamingo

The goal of this exercise is to practice dynamic balance, which means starting in motion, stopping in balance, and then resuming movement.

## Flaming Stance

- One leg is straight, but the knee is not locked.
- The other leg is raised, forming a “4” shape.
- The raised foot is in contact with the straight leg pointing down.
- Your weight is evenly distributed on the foot in contact with the ground.
- Your spine should be straight; imagine someone gently pulling a hair from the crown of your head.
- Your head, chest, hips, knee, and foot should all point in the same direction.
- Keep your head in a neutral position (don’t look down).
- Engage your core.
- Keep your elbows in contact with your body.
- Keep your hands in contact with your chet/belly.

## Stage 1: Standing Still

1. Stand with your feet parallel, shoulder-width apart, look straight ahed.
2. Shift your weight onto one leg.
3. Bring the free leg in, it should touch the other foot, and lift it off the ground.
4. Once you are in perfect balance, count three slow seconds.
5. Place the leg back in the starting position and repeat with the other leg.

*Notes:*
- Once you are comfortable, experiment with different heights for the leg that is lifting.
- To increase the difficulty, perform the same exercise with your eyes closed.
- To increase the difficulty, perform the same exercise straight, locked knees.
- If you intend to dance in heels, practice this exercise wearing them. The shoes influence your weight distribution.
- Focus on the contact of your feet to the ground.

## Stage 2: Movement to Balance

1. Take several steps forward.
2. As you step forward, gently bend the front leg while bringing the back leg in (it should touch the front leg), then lift the back leg.
3. Once you achieve balance, count three slow seconds.
4. Resume walking.

*Notes*
- Make sure you practice on both legs.
- To increase the difficulty, perform the same exercise with your eyes closed.
- Once you are comfortable with walking forward, practice walking backward.
- Always remember to bring the leg in before it goes up.

## Stage 3: Rotation

1. Stand on one leg.
2. Perform a 90-degree chest turn.
3. Step to the side with the raised leg, as in stage 2.

*Notes*
- Make sure your entire body is aligned properly.
- Make sure you bring the leg in before lifting it up.

## Stage 4: Dynamics

Execute any of the previous stage, but add the following variations:

- Take bigger or smaller steps.
- Change the speed. Step fast or slow.
- Add jump to Flaming Stance.
- Practice it on music following the tempo.

## Stage 5: Flamingo Away

Combine all previous stages, for example:

- Walk forward, then step to the side into Flamingo Stance.
- Walk backward, then step to the side into Flamingo Stance.
- Walk forward or backward in a circle and transition into Flamingo Stance.
- From Flamingo Stance, transition into another Flamingo Stance on the other leg:
    - Move forward, backward, or to the side.
    - Add rotation.
